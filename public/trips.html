How a trip works in Trezzit.

  1. One group for the trip.

    Any group size. Add friends by name; they join with an invite link when they’re ready.

  2. Every bill, by item.

    Tap who had what. Tax follows each item; the tip splits equally or in proportion.

  3. Settle in fewer payments.

    Turn on Simplified and debts combine. Nobody’s total changes.

Questions.

How do I split a dinner bill with tip?

Give each item to whoever had it. Tax follows each item. The tip splits equally, or in proportion to what each person ordered, with one tap.

Can I scan the receipt?

Yes. When you scan a receipt with AI features on, the photo is sent to Google Gemini to read it, and a small sample of scans is reviewed with Anthropic’s Claude to improve accuracy. AI features stay off until you turn them on. See the privacy policy.

Do my friends need Trezzit?

Not to start. Add them by name and split with them right away. When they’re ready, they join with an invite link, and the bills you added with them move to their account.

What currency does a trip use?

The one you pick when you create the group. Every bill in the group uses it, and Trezzit doesn’t convert between currencies.

Does Trezzit move money?

No, and it never asks for a bank login. You pay each other however you already do, then record the payment in Trezzit.
