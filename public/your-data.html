Your data at Trezzit

The short version of our Privacy Policy.

  • We never sell your data or use it for ads. No ad trackers, no tracking you across other apps.
  • AI only if you say yes. Receipt reading and expense sorting use Google Gemini, and only after you turn on AI features. You can turn them off any time in Profile → Privacy & data.
  • No AI training on your data. The AI providers that work for us process it on our behalf under terms that don't allow training their models.
  • Your group sees what it needs. Members see the bills you share and your balance with them, not your full email address.
  • Delete means delete. Deleting your account removes your personal data. Shared bills stay for the others under "Removed User", and backups are gone within 93 days.
  • Encrypted in transit. Everything travels over HTTPS; receipts sit in private storage.
