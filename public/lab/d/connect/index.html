Connect in a minute.

  1. Have a Trezzit account. Sign up free
  2. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors.
  3. Choose Add custom connector, name it Trezzit and paste the server URL.
  4. Sign in to Trezzit, check what Claude is asking for, and select Allow access.
  5. In a chat, turn Trezzit on from the tools menu and ask away.

You stay in charge.

  • Never moves money. No bank or card access.
  • Sees your expenses, groups and balances. Never other members’ emails.

Good to know.

Can it scan a receipt photo?

Yes, when your assistant can run code with internet access to upload the photo. Otherwise, scan the receipt in the Trezzit app and ask about it afterwards.

Can it delete things?

Only a bill it added in the last 30 minutes, and only before anyone recorded a payment on it. Everything else is deleted in the app.

What does Trezzit keep about my chats?

Trezzit never sees your conversation, only the requests your AI sends. For each one it keeps which action ran, when, whether it worked and what it touched, not the words. See and clear it in Profile → Connected AI apps.
