The app

Everything you ask, you can tap.

The same Trezzit, in your browser on any phone or computer. These are recordings of the real app.

1 · Scan

Snap the receipt. Every line comes back.

Add expense, then Scan. Trezzit reads each item and checks it against the total.

Or ask your AI“Scan this receipt.”
2 · Split

Tap who shared each item.

Every item has its own split. Pennies land somewhere, so it always adds up: $4.06 three ways is $1.36, $1.35 and $1.35.

Or ask your AI“Split it with my roommates.”
3 · Save

See who owes what, then save.

Whoever paid gets the rest back. Everyone's share lands in their own spending.

4 · Understand

Your share of everything, in one list.

Personal and shared together, each row showing what it was of. Search any item.

Or ask your AI“What did I actually spend in October?”

See what you actually spent.
