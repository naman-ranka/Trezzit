Why there’s no bank link

A bank sees one line per payment: CORNER PHARMACY −$48.22. It can’t tell allergy relief from a birthday card, or your share of the rent from your roommates’.

Trezzit starts from the receipt instead. You add receipts and bills; a scan reads the items and prices, and each item gets a category. You never give Trezzit a bank login, and it never moves money.

What you see

  1. Your share of everything

    A bill you split counts only your part. A dinner a friend paid for still counts. Personal and shared, in one place.

  2. By category, by month

    See where it went this month, last month or any dates you pick.

  3. Every item, searchable

    Search “sunscreen” and find the line, not just the store.

Your receipts

Reading receipts and sorting items are AI features, and they stay off until you turn them on. When they’re on, a receipt photo is sent to Google Gemini to read it, under terms that don’t allow training on your data. Trezzit doesn’t ask for or store bank account or card numbers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Can I track spending without linking my bank?

Yes. You add receipts and bills, by scanning or typing them, and Trezzit keeps your share of each. There is no bank login, and Trezzit never moves money.

How does Trezzit sort items into categories?

With AI features on, Trezzit suggests a category for each item, and you can change any of them. With AI features off, you pick categories yourself.

Do shared bills count?

Only your share. Pay $100.00 for groceries where $70.00 was your roommates’, and Trezzit counts your $30.00. A dinner a friend paid for counts too, at your share.

What happens to my receipt photos?

When you scan a receipt with AI features on, the photo is sent to Google Gemini to read it. AI features stay off until you turn them on. See the privacy policy.

Is Trezzit free?

Yes. There is no paid plan.
