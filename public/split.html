Split your own bill

How it works

  1. Scan the receipt

    Take a photo, or upload the PDF the restaurant emailed. Trezzit reads the items, the prices, the tax and the tip. Or type the items yourself.

  2. Give each item to whoever had it

    Tap the people who shared each item. Tax and tip are spread in proportion to what each person had, and every share is worked out to the cent.

  3. Send one link

    Everyone opens it on their phone, sees their own share with the items behind it, and marks it paid. Nobody installs anything or signs up.

A worked example

Dinner for three at Taqueria Sol: $66.70 of food, $6.17 tax and a $12.00 tip.

ItemWho had itWith tax
Bean and cheese burritoYou$12.57
Veggie fajita tacosAlex$15.02
Mushroom quesadillaPriya$13.38
Chips and guacamoleAll three$9.78
EloteAll three$9.83
HorchataYou and Alex$8.19
Jamaica agua frescaPriya$4.10
TipIn proportion$12.00
Total$84.87

You pay $27.03, Alex $29.88 and Priya $27.96: $84.87, exactly the receipt. Each item’s tax follows whoever had it, and the tip follows what each person ordered, so Alex, who had the most, covers the most of it. A cent that doesn’t divide evenly goes to one person, never lost or doubled.

What the table sees

  • Their share. One number, what they owe or are owed, with the items, tax and tip behind it.
  • Mark as paid. They tap it once they’ve paid. Part of it counts too, and anyone can undo a mistake.
  • Everyone’s share. Who paid the bill and who has paid back, on one page for the whole table.

In a group chat, the link shows the place, the date and the total before anyone taps it. A shared bill stays up for 90 days after its last visit; sign in and save it to keep it for good.

Questions

How do I split a restaurant bill with friends?

Scan the receipt, or type the items. Tap the people who had each item. Trezzit works out what everyone owes, including tax and tip, and gives you one link to send to the table.

Can I split a bill without an app or an account?

Yes. Splitting a bill runs in the browser and needs no account, and the people you send the link to don’t need one either. You can sign in later to keep the bill.

How are tax and tip split?

In proportion. Tax follows each item, so it lands on whoever had it, and the tip is shared by what each person ordered. In the splitter you can also split the tip equally.

What if everyone ordered something different?

That is what splitting by item is for: two people can share a starter while a third pays only for their own main. In the splitter, any item can also be split by share, by percentage or by exact amount.

What happens to my receipt photo?

When you scan a receipt in the splitter, the photo is sent to Google Gemini to read it. The samples on this page are recorded scans and send nothing. See the privacy policy.
