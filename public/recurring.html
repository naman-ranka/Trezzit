How repeating bills work.

  1. Any bill can repeat.

    Weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. The split comes with it.

  2. Auto or Remind me.

    Auto adds it on the due date. Remind me waits for you to check it.

  3. Skip a month, or end it.

    Skip a month nobody paid. Ending keeps every month it already added.

Questions.

Can I split Netflix or Spotify with friends?

Yes. Add it to a group, split it, and make it repeat monthly.

Can roommates split utilities that change each month?

Yes. Set it to Remind me and check the amount before it’s added.

Do my friends need Trezzit?

Not to start: add them by name, and they join with an invite link when they’re ready.

Is it only for shared bills?

No. A gym membership or a phone plan can repeat in your own spending, just yours.
