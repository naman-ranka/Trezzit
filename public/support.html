Trezzit support

Need help with Trezzit, or found something that isn't working? Email support@trezzit.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day.

Common questions #

How do I delete my account? Open Profile → Delete account in the app. Details on the account deletion page.

How do I turn off AI receipt reading? Open Profile → Privacy & data and switch off AI features. Everything else keeps working.

How do I report someone or something in a group? Email support@trezzit.com with the group name and what happened. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours.

Privacy questions? See our Privacy Policy and Your data at Trezzit, or email privacy@trezzit.com.
