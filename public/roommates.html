Questions.

How do I split rent by room size?

Split the rent by percent, such as 25, 25, 28 and 22. Any line can also be split equally, by shares or by exact amounts, so parking can go to one person while internet goes four ways.

Do my roommates need Trezzit?

Not to start. Add them by name and split with them right away. When they’re ready, they join with an invite link, and the bills you added with them move to their account.

Can rent add itself every month?

Yes. Make the bill repeat monthly and choose how each month arrives: it adds itself on the due date, or reminds you, so you check it and add it.

What if someone moves out?

They settle up and leave the group. Trezzit lets someone leave once their balance is zero, and the bills they were on stay with the group.

Does Trezzit move money?

No, and it never asks for a bank login. You pay each other however you already do, then record the payment in Trezzit.
